Monash Hub

Monash Hub puts what you usually dig through the Handbook and university pages for in one place. Every answer links its source and shows when it was last checked. The interface is in English and Simplified Chinese.

Open Monash Hubmonashhub.secureview.tech

What you can do

Section titled “What you can do”
Task Where
Look up a unit: campuses, teaching periods, assessments, exams, requisites, workload Search a unit code, or browse Units with filters
Find official information: WAM, special consideration, census dates, visas, exchange Search a keyword or question
See what a degree is made of and which units your campus actually teaches Degrees
Lay out units by semester and have each one checked against offerings and requisites Course map
Follow a unit back to what it needs, or forward to what it unlocks Unit tree
Work out WAM or GPA (and CGPA for the Malaysia campus) WAM/GPA calculator. Type unit codes, or paste or screenshot your WES results
Check Malaysia tuition fees for each course, whether per year or for the whole course, and the change since 2026 Malaysia fees
Check census, withdrawal, exams, results, Allocate+ and WES re-enrolment dates Key dates on the home page, with the full list under All key dates
Read a Chinese introduction for parents considering the Malaysia campus For parents in the header
Ask a quick question The Ask button. Answers come from the Handbook, official pages and student posts. It also shows replies from the 马莫百科 bot. When the bot has no ready answer, a free model may pick sentences from official pages, each checked word for word against its source
Read and share student experience Community. Sign in with an email address to post

The course map, unit tree and WAM/GPA calculator sit under Planning in the header.

Caution

Monash Hub is not run by Monash University. For enrolment, visas, assessment and academic policy, confirm on the linked official page, in Moodle or in WES.

Your data

Section titled “Your data”
  • Course maps are kept only in your browser. A check sends the plan to the server, which compares it with the Handbook and keeps nothing. Use Copy plan code in the More menu to move a plan to another device. The plan is written after the link’s #, which the browser never sends to the server. Open the link there, or use Paste plan code. Export still saves a file.
  • Marks are worked out in your browser. Only the unit codes go to the server, to look up credit points and level.
  • Ratings need no account.
