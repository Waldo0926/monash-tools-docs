I am Waldo, a computer science student at Monash. I built these tools for my own study first, then cleaned them up so other students can use them. More about how they were built is on [my blog](https://blog.secureview.tech).

## Rules every tool follows

- **Read-only by default.** Nothing submits, attempts or posts for you. The attendance helper is the only exception, and it waits for you to confirm each class.
- **Your login stays with you.** Tokens and cookies stay on your computer or in your browser.
- **Say so when unsure.** The Q&A tools quote sources and link them, and pass a question on rather than guess.
- **Free.** No paid tier, ads or tracking.

## Not official

These tools are not made, endorsed or supported by Monash University, Ed or Anthropic. Monash, Moodle and Ed belong to their owners. Always check anything important on the official page.
