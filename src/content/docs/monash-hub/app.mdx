---
title: Add Monash Hub to your home screen
description: Install Monash Hub like an app on iPhone, Android or a computer. Pages you have read work offline.
---

import { Tabs, TabItem, Steps, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

Monash Hub can sit on your home screen and open like an app. Units and guides you have already read still open without a connection. There is nothing to download from an app store.

<Tabs syncKey="device">
  <TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
    <Steps>

    1. Open [monashhub.secureview.tech](https://monashhub.secureview.tech) in **Safari** and tap **Share** (the square with an arrow). If you cannot see it, tap **···** next to the address bar first.
    2. Find **Add to Home Screen** in the menu.
    3. Tap **Add**.

    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Android">
    <Steps>

    1. Open the site and open the browser menu (**⋮** or **≡**).
    2. Choose **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**. The browsers on Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO and vivo phones may call it **Add to desktop**.
    3. If nothing happens, allow the browser to create home screen shortcuts in your phone's settings.

    </Steps>
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Computer">
    In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right of the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Inside WeChat">
    WeChat cannot add sites to the home screen. Tap **···** at the top right, choose **Open in Browser** or **Open in Safari**, then follow the steps for your phone.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside>
When you are offline, Monash Hub shows a saved copy with a notice that it may be out of date.
</Aside>
