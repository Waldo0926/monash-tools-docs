[Monash Hub](https://monashhub.secureview.tech) puts what you usually dig through the Handbook and university pages for in one place. Every answer links its source and shows when it was last checked. The interface is in English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

<LinkCard title="Open Monash Hub" description="monashhub.secureview.tech" href="https://monashhub.secureview.tech" />

## What you can do

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Look up a unit: campuses, teaching periods, assessments, exams, requisites, workload | Search a unit code, or browse **Units** with filters |
| Find official information: WAM, special consideration, census dates, visas, exchange | Search a keyword or question |
| See what a degree is made of and which units your campus actually teaches | **Degrees** |
| Lay out units by semester and have each one checked against offerings and requisites | **Course map** |
| Follow a unit back to what it needs, or forward to what it unlocks | **Unit tree** |
| Work out WAM or GPA (and CGPA for the Malaysia campus) | **WAM/GPA calculator**. Type unit codes, or paste or screenshot your WES results |
| Ask a quick question | The **Ask** button. It answers from the Handbook, official pages and student posts, with no AI |
| Read and share student experience | **Community**. Sign in with an email address to post |

## Know what you are reading

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Official Handbook` | Structured data from the Monash Handbook |
| `Official source` | Curated from official Monash pages |
| `Community` | Student questions and experience, not policy |

<Aside type="caution">
Monash Hub is not run by Monash University. For enrolment, visas, assessment and academic policy, confirm on the linked official page, in Moodle or in WES.
</Aside>

## Your data

- **Course maps** are kept only in your browser. A check sends the plan to the server, which compares it with the Handbook and keeps nothing. Use **Export** to move a plan to another device.
- **Marks** are worked out in your browser. Only the unit codes go to the server, to look up credit points and level.
- **Ratings** need no account.
