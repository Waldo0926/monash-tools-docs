Moodle, Ed, Mailand Attendance,answered by your AI.

Study Kit copies your own Moodle and Ed to your laptop, plus your school mail and attendance if you connect them, and lets the AI app on your computer search them: Claude, Codex (also inside the ChatGPT desktop app), Cursor and others. Ask what is due, what changed on Ed, which slide covers a topic, which emails need you, or which classes you have not checked in to. Your login stays on your computer, and the AI only gets what it looks up.

One question,one page read.

  1. 01

    Sync

    Every hour while your AI app is open, new files, Ed posts and replies are copied to your computer. Connected mail syncs every 30 minutes and attendance follows your timetable. Nothing is posted to Moodle or Ed, and mail or check-in waits for your OK.

  2. 02

    Index

    Slides, PDFs, notes, lecture transcripts and mail attachments go into a full-text index, in a folder only your user account can read.

  3. 03

    Find

    Your question is searched locally. The result is a file and a page number, like page 14 of the Week 8 slides.

  4. 04

    Answer

    The AI reads that one page instead of the whole PDF. On a real course search that used about 10% of the tokens.
