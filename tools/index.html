01Study Kit

Moodle and Ed, inside your AI app.

Syncs your own Moodle and Ed to your computer, read-only, and gives your AI app 19 tools to search them. Since 1.5.0 you can also connect your school mail and attendance (32 tools with both). Anything it changes there, such as a draft or a check-in, waits until you confirm the full preview. The same data works from the terminal with no AI at all.

02Monash Hub

Units, degrees and marks, in one site.

Look up a unit, see what a degree is made of, lay out a course map that is checked against offerings and requisites, work out WAM and GPA, and check Malaysia tuition fees and key dates. Every answer links its official source.

03Ed Web

An Ed forum you can skim.

Fetches your Ed forums into your own browser tab. Each post gets a one-line takeaway, staff answers are marked, and a whole unit's lesson files download as one zip. No server, no AI.

04Attendance Helper

It finds the codes. You confirm.

A Chrome extension that reads your classes from Monash Attendance and looks for the codes in Gmail, Ed and Moodle, including codes posted as screenshots. Nothing is submitted until you tick each class you attended.

05WeChat Q&A

Ask in Chinese. Get the official link.

Message the 马莫百科 WeChat account. Answers come from replies the owner wrote, Monash Hub and the account's own articles, each with a link. When it has nothing reliable, it says so and passes the question to a person.
